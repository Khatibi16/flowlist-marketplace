Start local, learn fast, scale across Milan

Partner with low‑to‑no‑digital‑presence clothing shops, digitize inventory for free, prove value, then expand.

Who we target

Segment: Independent clothing boutiques and surplus outlets in Milan with minimal online presence.
Pain: Slow turnover, manual listing, no discovery, limited footfall.
Beachhead areas: Navigli, Porta Venezia, Isola, and student‑dense zones.

Value exchange (pilot incentive)

Free digitization: We photograph items and generate listings (title, description, tags, price).
No‑risk setup: Shops keep the dataset; we take a success fee only on sales (post‑pilot).
Insights: Weekly report on demand, pricing, and trending categories.

Acquisition channels

Street outreach: Founder‑led visits, before/after demo on phone.
Local partners: Municipal retail associations; fashion schools for interns.
WhatsApp groups: Merchant communities; concise one‑pager + booking link.
Pop‑up digitization days: On‑site photo + listing sprints.

Pilot plan

Phase 1

Recruit a few Milan boutiques with low digital presence.

Phase 2

Free digitization: photos → AI listings (titles, tags, suggested price).

Phase 3

Observe operations, collect feedback, refine pricing and workflow.

Phase 4

Expand to nearby districts; formalize value‑based incentives.

Signals of success & risks

Signals: Repeat uploads; shops using AI suggestions without prompting; referrals.
Adoption: Owners can list independently with our simple playbook.
Risks: Low shop bandwidth; photo quality; price mismatch.
Mitigation: On‑site crew, simple playbook, price floors, light SLA.
